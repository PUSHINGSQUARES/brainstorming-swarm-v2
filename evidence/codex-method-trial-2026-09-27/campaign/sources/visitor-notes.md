# Synthetic Visitor Notes

1. Six of 12 visitors shown the current site map pointed to the east fountain when asked for the nearest refill point from the south gate. The east fountain is not shaded.
2. Four visitors said they would follow a sign with a water symbol and a large arrow before reading a paragraph of directions.
3. Two wheelchair users said the short path drawn through the lawn is not step-free. The marked paved route from the south gate takes an estimated four extra minutes at a comfortable pace.
4. One visitor asked whether a refill point would still be open after the evening performance. No operating hours were given in this interview packet.
