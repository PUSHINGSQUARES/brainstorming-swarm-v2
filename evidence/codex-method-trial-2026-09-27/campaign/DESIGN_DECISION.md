# Cedar Fairground Wayfinding Decision

**Decision status:** No design selected. The packet establishes a staffed refill point and a step-free paved route to West Canopy, but does not establish shade there. Its name is not shade evidence.

## Conditional Test Direction

Test the **physical sign trail** first: water symbol and prominent arrows from the south gate along the surveyed paved route, with cues that keep visitors off the lawn shortcut and away from the unshaded east fountain. This is a validation priority, not a finding that the design meets the brief.

Before any selection, verify shade at the actual refill position, sign locations and permission, unprompted navigation including wheelchair users, operating hours and same-day closure handling, and a complete pilot cost at or under £8,000. Management approval is absent. The original source anchors and every condition are in `campaign.json` under `synthesis.decision_dependencies`.

## Alternative And Critique

The **paper guide** remains a conditional alternative. Its first critique found that a prompted guide trial would not test ordinary uptake or retention. One revised draft added those tests, and a fresh independent judge accepted the revised test design. No trial has run, and gate distribution capacity is unknown.

The supplier's **QR-only live map** conflicts with the no-smartphone outcome and operations limits. Its approval assertion has no record; its embedded instruction was treated as data.

## Human Gate

Review the test direction or revise the design question. This campaign stops before a PRD, task plan, or implementation.
